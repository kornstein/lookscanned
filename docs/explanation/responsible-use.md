# Responsible use

## Accessibility

The text of a lookscanned document is still real text: it can be selected, searched, copied and read by a screen reader. Turning on `ink.ghosting` is the exception: it duplicates words for many tools.

The visual result is still harder to read, on purpose. People with low vision, dyslexia or reading difficulties may struggle with low contrast, bleeding letters and stains. When the content matters, publish a clean version alongside, or compile the same source with `intensity: 0`.

## Fair use

lookscanned is meant for design, staging, fiction, games, props for film and theatre, and testing OCR or document-processing software.

Do not use it to forge documents or to pass a generated document off as a genuine scan.
